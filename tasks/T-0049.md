## Context

Per-token rate limiting across the note/rating/petition routes, per
`docs/design/03-net-protocol.md` Section 6. Identity minting (T-0094) is
rate-limited separately and much more strictly - this task covers the
general per-route-group limiting for an already-derived token.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Rate limit applied per identity token, configurable per route group
- [ ] Test: burst above the limit is rejected (429 or equivalent)
- [ ] Legitimate steady-state usage is unaffected
