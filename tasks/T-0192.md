## Context

HANDOFF §20-a1. T-0187 through T-0190 landed the side-on sensor runtime
(SightConeSensorV2, SoundRadiusSensorV2, ProximityPatrolSensorV2,
CoverBreakV2, HidingSpotV2). This card rebuilds the §16-a one-room blockout
on top of those systems so it exercises the real side-on runtime rather than
the deprecated top-down objects. The rebuilt blockout is the prerequisite for
the §20-a2 measurement card (T-0193).

Grounded in `docs/design/14-vertical-slice.md` §16-a (one-room blockout
scope: grey boxes only, no chroma, no audio, no notes) and
`docs/HANDOFF.md` §20-a1.

## Story

As the game designer, I want the §16-a one-room blockout rebuilt on the
T-0187-T-0190 side-on sensor runtime — with the Watcher controller wrapping
all three sensors, the room coordinator constructing the layout in code, and
a minimal scene wiring them together — so that the spatial relationships
between player spawn, cover, hiding spot, item anchor, patrol zone, and
item-locked door can be exercised and measured in §20-a2.
