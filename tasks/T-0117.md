## Context

`02-notes-system.md` §6 (Broadcast Petition, unique tier): a petition
does not anchor to a place, it broadcasts to many players' worlds at
once. This is the enforcement mechanism for **INV-8**
(`08-invariants.md`) — reachability as an actual affordance, not an
arithmetic hope. **N-5 resolved:** a petition may name a gating (unique)
item, making it the real anti-lockout valve. Rare by construction —
"rate-limited per player" (§6), reinforced in `15-server-ops.md` §7
("tighter for petitions - broadcast is powerful").

`POST /v1/notes/petition {template_id, slots[]} -> 201 {id}` already
exists in `03-net-protocol.md` §2. **No `GET /v1/petitions` route is
currently documented** — broadcast notes (`is_broadcast = true`,
`04-data-model.md` §5) presumably surface through some fetch path, but
`03` doesn't specify one distinct from the anchored `GET /v1/notes`.
This card includes designing that read path, not just wiring the
already-documented POST.

## Story

As the server, I want anchorless broadcast petitions with a hard,
tighter-than-normal rate limit, so that the unique-tier "ask the
network" affordance stays rare and powerful rather than becoming
ambient noise.

## Acceptance

- [ ] `POST /v1/petitions` (or the existing `/v1/notes/petition`,
      reconciled to one canonical route) requires the unique vocabulary
      tier and creates an `is_broadcast = true` note with no anchor
- [ ] A petition may name a gating unique item type (N-5) as the thing
      being asked for
- [ ] `GET /v1/petitions` (new) surfaces active broadcast notes across
      the network — this route does not currently exist in
      `03-net-protocol.md` and needs its shape decided as part of this
      task
- [ ] Rate limiting on petition creation is strictly tighter than the
      general per-token rate limit (`03` §6, `15-server-ops.md` §7) —
      exact cost/cooldown is open tuning per `02` §6, implement against
      a configurable limit rather than a hardcoded one
- [ ] Test: exceeding the petition-specific rate limit is rejected even
      when the caller is well within the general per-token limit
- [ ] Test: a petition without the unique vocabulary tier unlocked is
      rejected (403, matching the authorization-failure pattern used
      elsewhere for tier gating in `03` §2)
