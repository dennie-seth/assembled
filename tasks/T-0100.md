## Result — name-collision check (2026-08-04, by Claude)

**Steam:** No standalone game titled exactly "Assembled." A store search for `assembled` returns many pages, but the namespace is crowded with near-identical titles — closest is **"Meta: Assembled"** (2019, free, app 1118640). Also present: *Assembly*, *Assemble!*, *Assemble with Care*, *The Assembly*, *Assembly Line 2*, several *…Heroes Assemble* titles, and the publisher **Assemble Entertainment**. The exact single word "Assembled" appears unclaimed as a standalone store title.

**itch.io:** No exact standalone "Assembled." Closest is **"Assembled In Orbit."** An `assembly` tag and **"The Game Assembly"** (a game-dev school account) are also present.

**Assessment:** "Assembled" looks available as an *exact* title on both stores, but discoverability/SEO will be hard given the crowded *Assemble/Assembly* space and the near-exact *Meta: Assembled*. If discoverability matters, consider a distinctive qualifier or a more unique title. This is not legal/trademark advice — do a USPTO/EUIPO search before committing to branding.
