## Context

`docs/design/13-asset-pipeline.md` §6.9 defines two concept-sheet
requirements that are cheap to approximate mechanically: the squint test
(what survives downscale to 16px is what has to be legible) and
deliberate value separation (value study before colour study, so
palette extraction in §6.10 has something to cluster).

This is **advisory, not a hard gate** — unlike the §2 validation gate for
shipped assets, a concept sheet has no fixed pass/fail bar; a human still
makes the call at concept review (§6, gate 1 of 2). The point is to give
that reviewer numbers alongside the image, not to block on them.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Squint-legibility proxy: downscale the sheet to a 16px-equivalent
      scale and compute an edge-density metric; surface the number, don't
      assert a pass/fail threshold that blocks review
- [ ] Value-spread proxy: value-channel histogram of the sheet, reported
      as a spread/coverage number (flags the "narrow mid-value band"
      failure mode called out in §6.10's round-1 assessment)
- [ ] Both numbers are surfaced to the human reviewer at concept-review
      time (§6 gate 1), not wired into any automated accept/reject path
- [ ] Unit-tested against known-good and known-bad fixture images (e.g. a
      flat high-contrast sheet vs. a hazy narrow-value one) to confirm
      the metrics move in the expected direction
- [ ] Explicitly out of scope: turning this into a hard gate. If it later
      proves reliable enough to block on, that's a separate task.
