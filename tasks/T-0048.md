`docs/HANDOFF.md`, but a genuine conflict was found while reconciling
PLAN.md against the new schema.** The original scope was a weighted RNG

GDD's rarity-cap `item_instance` model (`docs/design/04-data-model.md`
Section 3, decisions D-7/D-8) removes those tables entirely - rarity is a
hard instance-count cap enforced by the spawner, not a drop probability
rolled per-request, and quitting scatters items rather than draining them.

placeholder for whatever `07-items-economy.md` (pending delivery)
specifies as the actual "how does a player receive a new item" mechanism -
rescope once that doc lands. See `docs/design/03-net-protocol.md` Section 5
("Superseded").
