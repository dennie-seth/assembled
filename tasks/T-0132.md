## Context

Round 2 reported a candidate "P=20 floor" for the shard population, but
that number was derived without sweeping the rarity-cap constants (`k_c`,
`k_r`, `unique_count` from `07-items-economy.md` Section 2, open question
E-4) alongside population. Three seeds at P=30 in the transition zone
already showed an 8x spread (0.024 to 0.192), which means the floor is not
a stable constant -- it is a function of the unswept constants. Writing
"P=20" into `15` Section 3 as a fixed number would be premature and wrong.

## Story

As a simulation author, I want `k_c`, `k_r`, and `unique_count` swept
jointly with the population sweep, so that the shard population floor is
reported as a function of these constants rather than a single number.

## Acceptance

- [ ] `k_c`, `k_r`, and `unique_count` are swept jointly with the existing
      population sweep (not held fixed at Round 1/2 defaults)
- [ ] The population floor is reported as a function of `k_r` and
      `unique_count` (e.g. a table or surface), not a single scalar
- [ ] **The "P=20 floor" from Round 2 must NOT be written into `15`
      Section 3 until this sweep lands -- it is contingent on these
      unswept constants, and 3 seeds showed an 8x spread (0.024 to 0.192
      at P=30) in the transition zone.**
