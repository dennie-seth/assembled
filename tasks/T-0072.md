## Context

**Unblocked per `docs/HANDOFF.md` Section 3** - art direction is locked
(D-13: pixel art, 384x216, 16:9, integer-scaled, Soviet brutalism). Curate
30-50 reference images and train an SDXL style LoRA against them. Corpus:
**abandoned Soviet constructivism/brutalism** - concrete, institutional
geometry, repetition as architecture, buildings designed to outlast the
people in them (`docs/design/05-art-direction.md` Section 1).

This reference was chosen for the fiction, but per `05-art-direction.md`
Section 2 it happens to fit every pipeline constraint already in place -
worth keeping in mind while curating so refs lean into these properties
rather than fighting them:

- **Modular repetitive geometry** - a brutalist facade *is* a tileset;
  curate refs that show that repetition clearly
- **Large consistent corpus available** - curation should be easy, not a
  hunt; if a candidate ref feels like an outlier, it probably is one
- **Inherently desaturated** - the home palette (T-0073/V-5) should fall
  out of the reference set, not be imposed on top of it afterward
- **Forgiving of generation artifacts** - weathered concrete and decay
  read as texture, not error; don't over-filter refs for "cleanliness"
- **Hard geometric silhouettes** - side-on readability at 384×216 depends
  on clean shapes surviving downsampling; avoid organic/soft-edged refs
