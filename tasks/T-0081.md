## Context

**Revised per `docs/HANDOFF.md` Section 3.** Local install of Stable
Audio Open, **scope narrowed to textures only**: entity vocalizations,
room events, drones. Short percussive one-shots (~0.2s - footsteps,
switches, doors, pickups) move to **T-0101** instead, since diffusion
models are weak at that duration; do not attempt them here.

stage.

AudioGen excluded) - the repo is public and NC would poison forks.
