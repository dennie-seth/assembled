## Context

New task from `docs/HANDOFF.md` Section 4 - **the important one**. A
standalone simulation (a few hundred lines, no engine, no server -
plausibly lives under `tools/sim/` rather than inside `server/`) modelling
agents that join/play/idle/quit while items spawn/bleed/transfer/scatter,
with all four wall-clocks (`docs/design/10-time-and-progression.md`
Section 2) modelled together per `docs/design/08-invariants.md` Section 4.

**Build this now.** Its tuning parameters are blocked on @DennieSeth
(`docs/archive/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`: T-1, T-2, E-7, E-1) but its structure is
not. Its assertions are the same predicates as T-0096's two-player test -
one definition (INV-6...9, INV-14), three consumers (unit tests,
simulation, production monitoring).

**The four clocks to model** (`10-time-and-progression.md` Section 2):

| Clock | Scope | Range | Open param |
|---|---|---|---|
| Held bleed | item in inventory | 60-90 min (≈2× run) | E-1 |
| World/escrow bleed | item at an anchor | 48-72 h | E-1 |
| Unlock decay | `(variant_id, tag)` | minutes -> long, tiered | T-3 |
| Collapse | identity | weeks | T-1 |

**Rarity model to spawn against** (`docs/design/07-items-economy.md`
Section 2, D-7) - a hard instance-count *cap*, not a drop probability:
common `k_c·P`, rare `k_r·P` with `k_r ≪ k_c`, unique a fixed small
absolute count exempt from population scaling. Supply shrinks only via
landing-probability failure on bleed under over-supply
(`07-items-economy.md` Section 4) - there is no confiscation mechanic to
model, just a probability that a bleeding instance doesn't land.

## Results

The nine `08-invariants.md` §4 sweeps existed and ran (`sim = "sim.sweeps:main"`) but only
as fixed-config scenario checks — no run had ever exported a results artifact, and none
of them swept the open T-1/T-2/E-1 tuning brackets. Added `sim.run` (`python -m sim.run`,
tests in `test_run.py`) to grid-sweep those brackets at full uncompressed time scale and
export the results.

Full findings, tables, and reproduction steps: **[`tools/sim/results/RESULTS.md`](../tools/sim/results/RESULTS.md)**
(data: [`tuning_sweep.json`](../tools/sim/results/tuning_sweep.json) /
[`tuning_sweep.csv`](../tools/sim/results/tuning_sweep.csv), seed 42).

Headline: **INV-6 (rarity cap) never violated** across all 38 runs spanning both
brackets — the spawner/cap-enforcement is robust. **Held-bleed duration (E-1) is the one
parameter with real, actionable signal** — the fast end of its bracket (60–75 min)
cuts gating-type extinction under hoarder stress to ~6–9% of ticks vs. ~101–106% at the
range's slow/full end, while world-bleed duration shows no measurable effect.
**T-1 (collapse duration) and T-2 (unique-unlock decay) show no differentiating signal
anywhere in their brackets** — T-2 is currently invisible to every checked invariant, and
INV-8's per-tick violation rate stays flat (~51–53/tick) regardless of collapse duration.
Recommend holding both at their documented bracket midpoints (3 weeks / 7 days) absent a
reason to move.
