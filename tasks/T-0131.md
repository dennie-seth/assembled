## Context

Round 2 found `check_inv8` sitting at roughly 93% of ticks across nearly
every sweep condition, which is not a meaningful signal -- it reads as an
anchored-count test rather than a measure of whether unique items are
actually reachable. Held-but-circulating items (still in play, being
carried toward delivery) were being counted as unreachable, which is
wrong: reachability should track expected time-to-delivery against the
identity's remaining runway before collapse, not a static count.

## Story

As a simulation author, I want `check_inv8` rewritten to estimate expected
time-to-delivery for a unique versus the identity's remaining collapse
time, instead of an anchored-count test, so that INV-8 responds to actual
collapse proximity and held-but-circulating items are not misclassified
as unreachable.

## Acceptance

- [ ] `check_inv8` estimates expected time-to-delivery for held/circulating
      uniques and compares it against the identity's remaining time before
      collapse, rather than performing a static anchored-count check
- [ ] Held-but-circulating uniques do NOT count as unreachable solely for
      being currently held
- [ ] INV-8 no longer sits flat at ~93% of ticks across sweep conditions --
      it visibly responds to collapse proximity
- [ ] A healthy pool with long runway before collapse reports near-zero
      INV-8 violations
