## Context

Iteration-2 of T-0151 ("Populate model_hash in asset provenance (currently null)"). T-0151 was marked done (PR #110) but the defect recurred: T-0209's real SDXL+LoRA concept-art generation shipped `model_hash: null` after T-0151 closed, because T-0151's enforcement only covered `comfy_client.pipeline.generate()` — a second writer, `concept.py`'s `generate_concept_lora()`, was never brought under it.

Diagnosed and fixed as HANDOFF §21, option A in PR #221 (`fix/model-hash-provenance-null`, merged to develop 2026-08-21). This card covers the 8 already-committed null-hash assets not covered by T-0212/T-0213/T-0214, shrinking the provenance baseline from 21 to 13. Five of the eight (the T-0201 signal_tower props) were subsequently regenerated through the committed cutout recipe by T-0221 / PR #237, which removed them from the baseline (21 to 16) and gave them a real `model_hash` plus a resolvable `generator` path; this branch therefore backfills only the remaining three it still owns (the T-0209/T-0210 concept sheets and the T-0153 tile sheet, 16 to 13) and verifies all eight. The remaining 13 entries (T-0198/T-0199/T-0200 paths) are owned by those in-flight cards and are explicitly out of scope here.

`docs/design/13-asset-pipeline.md` §2 (validation gate, T-0102). HANDOFF §21, T-0151.
