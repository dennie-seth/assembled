## Context

**Blocked on V-5** (`docs/design/05-art-direction.md` Section 5,
`docs/design/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`) - the home palette's exact colour count
and hex values aren't decided yet, and the palette-quantize step needs
them. **The cutout (BiRefNet) and upscale (Real-ESRGAN) steps around it
are fine to build now** - only the quantize step in the middle is
blocked. Consider building this task in two parts so the unblocked steps
aren't held hostage by the blocked one.

The palette **family** is already fixed even though the exact hex set
isn't (`05-art-direction.md` Section 3) - the quantize step should target
this family once V-5 lands:
- Concrete greys (the structural base, widest range)
- Oxide and rust (the only warm notes)
- Institutional green and ochre (interiors, corridors)
- Deep shadow (near-black, not black)

Foreign-universe items/entities render in a *different* palette entirely
(`origin_palette`, chroma-as-distance-from-home, `01-vision.md` Section 8)
- this task's quantize step is for the **home** palette only; foreign
chroma is a separate per-instance shader concern (A-4, not this task).
