## Context

Round 2's INV-14 (time-to-completion vs population) measurement was
unreliable because it lacked a well-defined exit/completion event
(T-0130), was contaminated by the flat/miscalibrated INV-8 reachability
check (T-0131), and used sweep constants that Round 2 itself flagged as
unswept and load-bearing (T-0132). This card re-runs the measurement once
all three are fixed.

## Story

As a simulation author, I want to re-run the INV-14 measurement across
`P` in `{2, 20, 200, 2000, 20000}` with at least 30 seeds per point, using
the exit condition, multi-universe identity, and fixed INV-8 reachability
check, so that median time-to-completion vs population is reported as a
curve with IQR bands and the monotonicity question is answered explicitly.

## Acceptance

- [ ] INV-14 is re-measured across `P` in `{2, 20, 200, 2000, 20000}` with
      >= 30 seeds per population point
- [ ] Results use the T-0130 exit condition, T-0129 multi-universe
      identity, and T-0131 fixed `check_inv8`
- [ ] Median time-to-completion vs `P` is reported as a curve with IQR
      bands (not a single point estimate per `P`)
- [ ] The monotonicity question (does time-to-completion monotonically
      increase/decrease with `P`, or is it non-monotonic) is answered
      explicitly in the report
