## Context

The archetype-first coherence guard from `docs/design/13-asset-pipeline.md`
§6: an archetype's first approved concept sheet should condition every
concept sheet (and eventually recipe) that follows for that archetype, so
a second or third sheet doesn't independently drift from the first. Right
now T-0104 has no way to feed a prior approved sheet back in — every
generation is an independent prompt.

This task adds that conditioning path to `AssetAgent`/`comfy-client` —
IP-Adapter or img2img seeded from the approved sheet, not a bare prompt —
and threads `concept_hash` (sha256 of the approved sheet, already captured
by T-0104's provenance) through to every asset generated under that
conditioning, the same way `workflow_hash` already rides in
`comfy_client.provenance`.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Given a prior approved concept sheet, a new concept (or recipe)
      generation can condition on it via IP-Adapter or img2img rather
      than prompt text alone
- [ ] `concept_hash` of the conditioning sheet is threaded through
      provenance for every asset generated under it
- [ ] Unit-tested against a mocked ComfyUI backend
- [ ] Documented choice of IP-Adapter vs. img2img (or both, situationally)
      and why
