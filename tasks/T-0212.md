## Context

Supersedes **T-0198** in place. T-0198 is not edited; it remains the
original run and stays `done` on the board. That run passed T-0102's
gate but was generated with no concept-art input -- `13-asset-pipeline.md`
§6's archetype-first coherence guard was not available for characters
until T-0106 (concept conditioning) landed, and no per-character concept
sheet existed at the time. This card re-runs the player idle sheet
conditioned on the new [Concept art -- Player character](T-0209) sheet.

`docs/design/13-asset-pipeline.md` §3.5 (Characters — the hard class):
at 384×216 a figure is 40px tall. Nearly all generated detail is
destroyed in the descent; what survives is shape and a few value blocks.

## Story

Character generation, player idle sheet, re-run. 40px figure, 48x48
cell, 3x3 grid, 144x144 native, gen at 1152x1152 (x8), fallback 1008
(x7). ControlNet pose grid, now IP-Adapter/img2img-conditioned on the
approved player concept sheet (T-0106's conditioning path) rather than a
bare prompt.
