## Story

As the game designer, I want the full seven-room Signal Tower traversal measured
using side-on sensor constants from the committed blockout — with per-room
time ranges, a critical-path total, and a comparison to the superseded DL-16
top-down estimates — recorded as decision-log entry DL-19, and open decision
E-1 (held bleed / world reset cadence) resolved and recorded as DL-20, so that
the project's time-and-progression design is grounded in observed data.
