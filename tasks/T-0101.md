## Context

New task from `docs/HANDOFF.md` Section 4. Diffusion models (Stable Audio
Open, T-0081) are weak at short percussive one-shots (~0.2s) - footsteps,
switches, doors, pickups. Instead, synthesize these deterministically in
`assets/src/` with seeded numpy/scipy: noise burst -> resonant filter ->
envelope, physically-inspired rather than sfxr-style chiptune.

This is why T-0081's scope narrowed to textures only (entity
vocalizations, room events, drones) - one-shots live here instead, fully
unblocked and needing no model at all (`docs/HANDOFF.md` Section 7).

## Acceptance

      pickups to WAV from a seed
- [ ] Test: same seed produces a byte-identical WAV output
- [ ] Output passes the loop-fold/seam and loudness checks from T-0102
